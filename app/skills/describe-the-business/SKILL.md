# Describe the business

A domain map carries one description of its own: what the business is. Every
other task reads it first, because it is what turns a review of "a map" into a
review of *this* business — so it is worth getting right before anything else
is asked. This map's is missing, or thin. Draft it from what is on the map, for
the owner to correct.

Write one short paragraph, under 600 characters, that says:

- **What the business is**, in a sentence a newcomer would understand: the
  industry and the business model. "A buy-now-pay-later provider: it pays
  merchants up front and collects from consumers in instalments."
- **Who it serves**: the customers and the other parties the map shows or
  implies — merchants, consumers, partners, regulators.
- **What this map covers, and what it leaves out.** A map of the lending
  business that leaves out HR and facilities should say so, so that nobody
  reports their absence as a gap.
- **Which published reference models apply**, if any plainly do — BIAN for
  banking, TM Forum for telecoms, APQC PCF across industries. Name one only if
  you are confident it fits; "none that I know of" is a good answer.

Work from the evidence: the map's title, the domains and what they hold, the
actors and touchpoints, what the lines carry. Where the map leaves something
open, say what you assumed in `why`, as a list the owner can go down and
correct — a wrong assumption stated plainly is more use than a vague paragraph
that cannot be wrong.

Write as the owner would, in the plain present, in the language of the map. No
selling, no "leading", no "seamless".

Return one suggestion holding one `describe` whose `target` is `"map"`. If the
map is too empty to tell what the business is, return a note asking for it
instead of guessing.

## Given and returned

`describe`. A suggestion with no operations is a note, and is always allowed.

If you have no brief, ask for one: **Assistant → Copy prompt** in the app
